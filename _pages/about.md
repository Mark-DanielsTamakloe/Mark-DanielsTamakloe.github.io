Hi, I’m Mark-Daniels Tamakloe, a Master’s student in Computer Science at Washington University in St. Louis. My academic interests lie at the intersection of  data science, machine learning, and causal inference. I focus on using causal mediation analysis to examine how race influences treatment outcomes, with the broader goal of leveraging advanced AI/ML methods to address healthcare disparities and inform equitable policy.

Beyond academics, I enjoy playing soccer and competing in FC25 on my PS5. The game offers a fun balance to my research life. I’m excited about the future, where technology, data, and causal reasoning combine to create more effective and inclusive healthcare solutions.
